## World land geometry

`data/maps/land.geojson` is derived from Natural Earth's low-resolution country geometry bundled as the `naturalearth_lowres` test fixture in the locally installed pyogrio environment. During preparation, 177 geometries were dissolved to land, then simplified with tolerance 0.16 degrees. Only land polygons were retained; country attributes and administrative outlines were discarded.

The prepared GeoJSON is bundled, so users do not need pyogrio, GeoPandas, Shapely or a download during build. It is not claimed to be a freshly downloaded or the latest Natural Earth edition. Generalized geography is a visual backdrop, not a survey-grade dataset or evidence of any visitor's location.

Natural Earth data is public domain. Official terms (checked 2026-10-15):
<https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>
